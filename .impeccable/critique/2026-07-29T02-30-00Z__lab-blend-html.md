## Design Specificity Verdict

**The chrome is authored; the default material answer is the generic part.** The panel, HUD, and labels are unmistakably Deliberate Machinery: mono machine voice throughout, hairline-bordered buttons (:79–84), one cyan for pressed/value states (:84, :92), square everything — and the distinctive move: every control group states the thesis its knobs test ("The word is a slab, not a sticker", :237–239; "Flux is the north star made literal", :291–293). No dat.gui genericism anywhere.

But the *effect* at defaults (mat=1 surface, blend=1 alpha, spec 1.1 — :446–450) renders headings as cyan foam flecked with per-grain speculars. "Density reads as luminosity" — the identity DESIGN.md builds three named rules on (Additive Ceiling, Glow Is Earned, Grain Ratio) — is suspended inside the very rig deciding what graduates. Any three.js demo has lit specular sprites; only this system has luminous conserved matter. The lab is specific; its current default is not. (See Question 1.)

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs` exit 2, exactly **1 advisory finding** — `design-system-color` for `#fff` at line 980. That is the `#dbg` debug overlay's text color; the file's other two `#fff` literals (:633 atlas mask, :814 offscreen rasterizer) are the documented false-positive family (alpha masks tinted cyan by the shader). Effectively a clean scan.

**Pixel evidence (B):** across destruction, retype, and idle frames — **0 pure-white pixels, 0.000% near-white**, every non-background cluster cyan-family (hue 185–190°). The One Hue Rule and Additive Ceiling hold in rendered output at the sampled moments (the manifest-swell blowout in issue 4 was observed by A at t≈2.3, a moment outside B's histogram set).

**Browser overlays:** not applicable — target is a `file://` lab page; evidence was gathered via deterministic headless captures (the project's own `#t0`/`#demo` instrumentation) rather than live-server script injection. No JS errors in console; the only stderr line is Chromium's SwiftShader deprecation notice.

## Overall Impression

This is the rare tuning rig that is itself designed. The panel reads as a design document, the lab photographs itself deterministically, and the caret-retype recovery is a genuinely great moment — the right default. But the instrument's calibration is visibly broken (furniture and matter disagree on screen), the destruction phase — the moment the visitor's own hand causes — is the *darkest* thing on the page instead of the most energetic, and the lab's actual deliverable (a tuned parameter state) evaporates on reload. The single biggest opportunity: make the furniture agree with the matter and make energy read as light; those two fixes carry the effect most of the way to the igloo/lusion bar.

## Priority Issues (ordered)

### 1. [P1] The instrument's calibration is broken — furniture and matter disagree on screen
**What:** Carets, labels, and vessel walls are all cut from seed-time `getBoundingClientRect` (:869–870, :954, :1013–1014, :985–986), the same rects the sampler maps grains from (:812–849) — yet on screen they disagree, and the error grows down the ramp. Corroborated independently by both assessments: retype carets float ~60px **above** the statement's glyph tops in two frames (a/d18, a/d20; b/demo1-t1.8, b/demo1-t2.0); A measured label-center vs matter-band offsets of +7 (display) → +46 (statement) → +56 (headline) → +63 (figure) → +74 (title) on idle3.png. Separately and definitively from source: the decode lattice is **viewport-global** (`ci = floor(pos/cw), cj = floor(pos[j+1]/chh)`, :1234–1236), so decode rows snap to an absolute grid and B captured a second glyph row rendering *below the statement's own box* in the gap above "Experience" (b/beh1-t0.9).
**Why it matters:** every alignment-sensitive judgment made in this rig — caret height against cap height, vessel wall distance, label placement — is being made against a mis-registered ruler. Nothing tuned here can be trusted at graduation into matter.js until furniture and matter agree.
**Fix:** root-cause the rect→canvas mapping (the `textBaseline:'middle'` at `ch/2` assumption, :814–820, vs the mapped-back grain position at :843–844) with a one-off ruler overlay; consider anchoring all fx/labels to the sampled occupancy's own y-bounds (min/max of `loc`, already computed at :832–849) so furniture can never drift from matter again. Anchor the decode lattice to each heading's own baseline instead of the viewport origin (offset `ci/cj` by the heading's rect). Caveat: the label-offset magnitudes were measured under headless SwiftShader with CDN fonts — re-verify in a real browser — but the caret float appears in both agents' frames and the global decode lattice is a source-level fact.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### 2. [P1] Destruction reads as darkening — the taffy signature is the muddiest thing on screen
**What:** The stretch/streak capsules (the throw mode's whole signature) render as dark teal wedges nearly vanishing into the vignette (a/d09; b/demo1-t0.9 "dark, desaturated wedge-shaped spikes much darker than the cyan grains", quantized to #212D30/#263D42). Cause: `tetLum = vLit * (0.42 + 0.58*cyl)` (:604) with tumbling loose normals (:1471–1477) driving `vLit` low, under the alpha-blend default where dark grains paint *darker than the field*.
**Why it matters:** energy input reads as light leaving the system — backwards for both physics credibility and the emotional beat. The moment the visitor's own hand causes is the cheapest-looking frame of the cycle; lusion-grade stretched matter stays luminous under strain.
**Fix:** give elongated capsules an emissive floor scaled by |velocity| (energy → light), or hold `vLit` at the grain's rest value while `vE > 0` (:609–611); verify in both blend modes. Ship the knob with it (see issue 8).
**Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

### 3. [P1] The lab's deliverable — a tuned state — evaporates
**What:** The hash is read (:463–465) but never written; no save, no reset, no copy-state. `r` reseeds geometry (:1882) but nothing restores or captures `P`. The site's own tweak panel has save/reset; the lab has neither.
**Why it matters:** the entire output of a tuning session is ~90 parameter values, currently capturable only by hand-reading the panel. This is the Every Knob Rule missing its other half, in the one place knobs are the product. Also the H3=1 driver.
**Fix:** serialize non-default keys of `P` to `location.hash` on every input (one ~6-line function reusing the existing hash-read format) — this simultaneously creates shareable version links, which are the lab's actual deliverable. Add a reset that reassigns the default `P` and re-runs the four `set*` calls + `seed()`.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### 4. [P2] The retype climax blows out achromatic at the peak-end moment
**What:** At the end of the caret pass, the manifest swell (`msize` 4.5, :455, applied :1562) stacking with the lead flash (`dlead`, :1563) and caret-pinned emission (`min(c.x, home+28)`, :1544) piles hot sprites into a white smear on the just-retyped "real." (a/d23; b/demo1-t2.3 saw the same region "blobby/turbulent"). B's histograms confirm the rest of the cycle is blowout-free, which makes this the one Additive Ceiling violation — at exactly the frame that is the second-to-last impression of every demo cycle.
**Why it matters:** peak-end rule — the caret retype is the best moment in the file, and it currently ends on its worst frame.
**Fix:** drop `msize` default toward ~2 (re-measure, per The Defaults Are The Measured Set Rule), or gate the swell by local emission density; decouple flash amplitude from size swell.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

### 5. [P2] The Vessel is undiscoverable and undemonstrable
**What:** Three compounding gaps. (a) No HUD slot: `setCont` (:1793–1797) changes nothing visible outside the panel — B confirmed `#cont=2` frames are indistinguishable from default in the HUD. (b) `b` blind-cycles three states (:1881) while its kbd hint sits on "Per word" only (:373), implying it selects that option. (c) The demo never exercises it: in no captured frame does a wall light or matter pool — at defaults the 0.3s heal delay (:451) plus the scan burn (:1446–1449) erase strays before gravity (380px/s², :450) can pool them, and walls only light above hard-coded 140/260 px/s impact speeds (:1461–1466). The newest physics group is invisible to the automated comparison pass that exists to compare versions.
**Why it matters:** a version switch you can't see the state of, and a behaviour the demo can't photograph, is effectively not in the lab.
**Fix:** add a `hud-cont` slot next to `hud-ret`; move the `b` hint to the Vessel `h2`; add one hard downward flick (or a space-shatter beat) to the demo script (:1348–1358) and let one cycle variant delay the scan so pooling is photographable.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

### 6. [P2] Rest state is foam, not conserved matter — the site's own Grain Ratio Rule broken at the defaults
**What:** At defaults (grain 1.6, :448; per-heading grid `fs/30` clamped 2–8, :810) sprites run well past the ~2× sample-spacing ratio DESIGN.md fixed after measuring this exact failure ("coverage bought past the point where sprites merge buys blur"). The name reads as bubble-foam with white specular flecks rather than discrete matter (a/idle3), and the 27px title row is mush in every frame — it sits below the site's 26px matter floor by design, but the lab presents it as a live candidate rather than saying so.
**Why it matters:** the rig's rest state is the baseline every behaviour is judged against; if the baseline is smeared, every comparison inherits the smear.
**Fix:** re-measure defaults at grain ≈1.1–1.2; annotate the title row's label with its verdict (below the floor — reference, not candidate).
**Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

### 7. [P2] Input robustness — the stress tester wins in ten seconds
**What:** (a) Hash values are coerced with `+v` and never clamped (:463–465): `#r=abc` → NaN poisons the Float32 buffers and the field silently vanishes; `#beh=7` leaves HUD "undefined" (:1771) and no behaviour group visible. (b) The keydown handler ignores modifiers (:1872–1888): Ctrl+R reseeds before reload, Cmd/Ctrl+1..3 swaps material. (c) After touching any slider, focus stays on the input and the guard at :1873 swallows x/c/z/v until the canvas is clicked — a dozen-times-a-day papercut for the one daily user. (d) The Material group under strands is dimmed `.inert` (:75, :1768) but still fully clickable, and keys 1–3 still work while dimmed — visual-only inertness.
**Fix:** clamp hash numerics to each control's min/max and enums to their range; bail on `e.metaKey || e.ctrlKey || e.altKey`; blur sliders on `change` (or route the key handler past range inputs); actually disable inert buttons.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### 8. [P3] Chrome drift from the design system and the every-knob policy
**What, in one sweep:** (a) Naming triangle: the button says "Scan + caret · retype" (:411), the HUD says "caret" (:1763), the keys legend says "return" (:432) — three names for one axis. (b) Sub-AA prose: explanatory copy, kbd hints, and role labels are set in `--dimmer` (40% ink, 3.39:1) at 10–10.5px (:45, :68, :85–88, :102) — the token DESIGN.md's AA Floor Rule reserves for non-text. (c) 10.5px is off the closed type ramp (:68, :92, :99). (d) `<title>` still says "strands, decode, form" (:6) — stale by two behaviours. (e) Several values new this session are hard literals with no panel control, against the standing every-knob instruction: scan opacity 0.35 (:1052), caret tilt 6deg (:1072) — also fixed rather than velocity-derived as the site's caret doctrine specifies — wall-hit thresholds 140/260 (:1461–1466), burn kick −90 (:1447), manifest caret offset 28 (:1544). (f) `#cost` (:99, :1781) shows a prose blurb, never a cost; an fps-per-mode figure would earn the name.
**Fix:** pick one name per Return option and use it in all three places; lift lab prose to `--quiet` (55%); retitle; add the five missing knobs or demote the values to derived expressions.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable typeset (copy/tokens) + /impeccable polish (knobs)

## Questions to Consider

1. **Lit solid or luminous matter?** Form/Light/Blend are a large bet on "the word is a slab" — specular, rim, extrusion, alpha occlusion — quietly abandoning "density reads as luminosity", the idea the site's three central rules are built on. The current default is neither: foam with white flecks. Decide the thesis before tuning further; every Light knob is spent on the losing one if the answer is "luminous."
2. **Does the site ever need the scanline?** Scan+caret exists to clear five simultaneously-wounded headings — a situation the four-page site (one heading set per viewport, one cursor) may never produce. If the caret alone suffices there, the scan is lab scaffolding being polished as product.
3. **Which ten knobs are still open questions?** The feature map is finalized; 90 live controls say otherwise. A lab that froze settled values into constants would show, by its own panel length, what is actually still being decided.
